Figure caption

ACT-Tracks with auxiliary motion learning. The policy receives a causal RGB window, current robot joints and a subset of observed 3D point histories. A shared ResNet-18 retains the native spatial grid for current images and pools past image features before adding spatial and physical-time encodings. A shared per-track MLP encodes current anchors, past displacements, timestamps and validity into point tokens. One gated cross-attention layer conditions the native ACT encoder memory on these tokens; the native ACT decoder predicts an action chunk. During training, a separate observation-only pass reuses the same observation modules with the latent fixed to zero. Two independent point-query heads predict past and future displacements of withheld points from their current XYZ anchors and signed query times. Withheld displacements are labels only, and auxiliary predictions are never fed into the policy. Future supervision learns expert continuation from observations, without future-command conditioning. Masked, scale-normalized Smooth L1 losses supervise the point heads. Their gradients regularize shared observation modules under a global norm budget; the ACT action decoder and CVAE posterior retain the policy objective. At inference, only the observation-to-action path is evaluated. The illustrated pilot uses oracle persistent-vertex tracks, including occluded past positions.

Presenter notes

We keep the native ACT action decoder and extend its observations with RGB history and 3D point tracks. A shared ResNet and a per-track MLP encode the two modalities, and one gated cross-attention layer conditions the ACT memory on motion. During training, a separate zero-latent observation pass feeds two auxiliary heads: past completion and future motion prediction. These heads regularize the shared representation and are omitted at inference. Future prediction learns expert continuation from observations. Our present point inputs are oracle simulator tracks.

Illustrated setting: 16 observed tracks, 48 withheld query points, a 16-frame history and a 100 × 12 action chunk. Input drawings and curves are schematic.
