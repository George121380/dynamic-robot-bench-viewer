How it connects

Observed history → encoder/trunk → features [B,L,256]
Current 3D points [B,N,3] ────────┐
                                ↓
PointFlowDecoder + 50 Euler steps
                                ↓
Normalized displacement [B,100,N,3]
                                ↓
Explicit training mean/std → metric trajectories

The original head uses HPT future query tokens computed from observations. Reusing pretrained weights with a new encoder requires adaptation.

What these checks establish

Synthetic tensors verify weight identity, sampling, masked loss, gradients, saving and resuming. They do not measure task accuracy. No task dataset adapter, closed-loop evaluation or official benchmark reproduction is claimed.

The public release has no separate flow statistics file. Metric predictions require explicit matching training statistics; new baseline training must fit them on its training split.
