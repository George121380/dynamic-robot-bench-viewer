LeHome demonstration viewer

Original code, assets and demonstration recordings: LeHome contributors.
Code: https://github.com/lehome-official/lehome-challenge
Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/lehome/dataset_challenge_merged
Dataset revision: 17e8dee8fac294ffd21d250501d3b31bf8679042

Official preview videos are complete fixed episodes extracted from the original
recordings and re-encoded as H.264 for browser playback. Numeric curves are rounded
to five decimal places. Visual review notes and this viewer were added for inspection.
Native replay videos, if present, are newly rendered simulation output and labelled
separately. No new demonstrations or autonomous-policy evaluations are claimed.
