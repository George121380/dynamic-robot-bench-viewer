## Settings pending user review

The effective recipe is `configs/flow-study-01.json` plus `FlowConfig` defaults. These are proposed settings, not approval to start.

- Inputs: every condition gets sixteen three-camera RGB frames and current measured 12D joints (no joint history). Geometry adds K=16 current XYZ; Tracks adds those points' past tracks; Completion has identical inference inputs to Tracks and 48 training-only hidden-point queries/targets.
- Timing: native 640×480 RGB, no crop/resize/augmentation; history stride three at 90 Hz spans 0.5 seconds. Missing history is masked. Window starts also have stride three, shuffled per seeded epoch. Predict 100×12 controls, execute ten before replanning at 9 Hz, without temporal ensembling.
- Architecture: ImageNet ResNet18 with FrozenBatchNorm statistics and trainable backbone weights; 4×4 spatial tokens per camera/frame plus spatial/camera/time embeddings. Main width 512, eight heads, FFN 3200, dropout 0.1; two shared-observation encoder layers, four actor encoder layers, one action decoder layer. Four-layer CVAE posterior, latent 32; sampled posterior in training and fixed z=0 in evaluation/inference.
- Points: width 256, four heads, FFN 1024; two temporal and two inter-point encoder layers, projection to 512; two-layer completion decoder reading shared observations only. Current-visible FPS selects 64 points before seeded random 16/48 partition. Selection is fixed per window, identities stay aligned within the window, and no motion-based sampling is used. Position scale 0.5m, displacement scale 0.05m; completion output starts with weight std=1e-4 and zero bias.
- Data: all 250 official fixed-revision action episodes rerendered synchronously. Train Seen_0..7 (200 episodes), validation Seen_8 (25), offline test Seen_9 (25), garment split before windows. All complete replays retain success labels without success filtering. State/action statistics use training data only, std floor 0.01; no formal training before the complete SHA inventory freezes.
- Optimization: four conditions × seeds 1000/1001/1002, 30,000 updates each. Single-GPU micro-batch one × accumulation sixteen = effective batch sixteen; 480,000 consumed windows per run. AdamW policy/backbone lr 1e-5, betas=(0.9,0.999), eps=1e-8, weight decay 1e-4; 500-update warmup then cosine to 0.1×initial lr, clip norm one. BF16, strict deterministic algorithms, two loader workers, four CPU threads; six-image backbone chunks, no backbone checkpointing, 16GiB process memory cap.
- Objectives/selection: valid normalized-action mean L1 + 10×KL for every condition; Completion alone adds 0.1×normalized hidden-past-displacement SmoothL1 (beta one). No future-track prediction. Every 500 updates, the same fixed 128 validation windows select best by prior-zero action L1; complete checkpoints every 1000, logs every 100. Final best is evaluated on all validation/test windows. No selection by test success or completion error.
- Closed loop: Seen_9 and Unseen_0/1 × ten pose seeds 2027..2036 × three training seeds × four conditions = 360 rollouts. Point seed 2026, paired initial states, 90Hz, 600-action cap, fifty actions after first success. Primary final success; secondary ever success; paired differences and 95% bootstrap intervals. Eight GPUs would parallelize independent runs without changing batch or using larger-batch DDP; scheduling remains pending approval of settings.

GT histories, including occluded past segments, are privileged simulation information rather than RGB-estimated optical flow. Information and active capacity differ from RGB-H; Tracks−Geometry and Completion−Tracks are the cleaner mechanism contrasts. The old single-frame ACT 14/20 is not the matched baseline. Six-step engineering checks do not establish convergence or useful completion.

RGB-H uses sixteen three-camera RGB frames and current measured 12D joints. Geometry adds K=16 current cloth coordinates; Tracks adds the same points' past motion; Completion adds only training supervision for 48 other past tracks. RGB-H never decodes GT arrays or opens the GT cache. The auxiliary head branches before the future-action CVAE posterior; hidden queries/labels/predictions cannot enter the actor, and the head is removed at deployment. Prior-zero evaluation follows the [official ACT formulation](https://tonyzhaozh.github.io/aloha/). The history and track encoders are custom extensions, not a layer-for-layer reproduction of the public single-frame checkpoint.

Only training data informed the numerical correction: 42,144 valid target vectors had median displacement 0.84 mm, mean 14.46 mm and p95 112.08 mm. Position remains normalized by 0.5 m; motion now has a separate fixed 0.05 m scale. Tiny output weights and zero bias initialize completion near static displacement while preserving nonzero encoder gradients. Auxiliary weight remains 0.1 and is not tuned on test outcomes. Legacy checkpoints retain their previous units and execution cadence.

Every formal condition predicts 100 actions but executes only the first ten before observing again. At 90 Hz this removes the previous 100-step open-loop commitment equally for all models. No temporal ensembling or model-specific replanning rate is introduced. Content-addressed read-only GT memmaps avoid repeated NPZ decompression without changing any values; derived bytes are hashed when opened. Cached/raw windows match exactly, and RGB-H never invokes this adapter.

The shared parameter skeleton does not make active capacity or FLOPs identical: RGB-H bypasses the track encoder. Geometry/Tracks share the active architecture, and Tracks/Completion share inference capacity. Overall Flow versus RGB-H changes both privileged inputs and their encoder, so the total difference cannot be attributed exclusively to temporal representation. Parameter groups and wall time are reported explicitly.

The principal baseline is newly trained RGB-H. The old public ACT score of 14/20 remains a pipeline reference.
