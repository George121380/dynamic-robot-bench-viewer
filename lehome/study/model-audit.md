# RGB-H / FlowACT design audit and matched study

The four conditions are suitable for a controlled simulation upper-bound experiment after fixing completion initialization, motion scaling and the common action execution horizon. Observation/label boundaries and causal alignment are checked. The durable study first completes synchronized replay, then runs training and paired evaluation automatically. No formal policy result exists yet.

RGB-H uses sixteen three-camera RGB frames and current measured 12D joints. Geometry adds K=16 current cloth coordinates; Tracks adds the same points' past motion; Completion adds only training supervision for 48 other past tracks. RGB-H never decodes GT arrays or opens the GT cache. The auxiliary head branches before the future-action CVAE posterior; hidden queries/labels/predictions cannot enter the actor, and the head is removed at deployment. Prior-zero evaluation follows the [official ACT formulation](https://tonyzhaozh.github.io/aloha/). The history and track encoders are custom extensions, not a layer-for-layer reproduction of the public single-frame checkpoint.

Only training data informed the numerical correction: 42,144 valid target vectors had median displacement 0.84 mm, mean 14.46 mm and p95 112.08 mm. Position remains normalized by 0.5 m; motion now has a separate fixed 0.05 m scale. Tiny output weights and zero bias initialize completion near static displacement while preserving nonzero encoder gradients. Auxiliary weight remains 0.1 and is not tuned on test outcomes. Legacy checkpoints retain their previous units and execution cadence.

Every formal condition predicts 100 actions but executes only the first ten before observing again. At 90 Hz this removes the previous 100-step open-loop commitment equally for all models. No temporal ensembling or model-specific replanning rate is introduced. Content-addressed read-only GT memmaps avoid repeated NPZ decompression without changing any values; derived bytes are hashed when opened. Cached/raw windows match exactly, and RGB-H never invokes this adapter.

The shared parameter skeleton does not make active capacity or FLOPs identical: RGB-H bypasses the track encoder. Geometry/Tracks share the active architecture, and Tracks/Completion share inference capacity. Overall Flow versus RGB-H changes both privileged inputs and their encoder, so the total difference cannot be attributed exclusively to temporal representation. Parameter groups and wall time are reported explicitly.

The principal baseline is newly trained RGB-H. The old public ACT score of 14/20 remains a pipeline reference. Predeclared contrasts separate current geometry (Geometry−RGB-H), additional motion (Tracks−Geometry), auxiliary supervision at identical inference inputs (Completion−Tracks), and the total privileged-input effect (Completion−RGB-H).

All four use the same 16-frame/0.5-second history, native 640×480 RGB from three cameras, current joints, 100×12 predictions, ten-step execution prefix, ImageNet weights, training-only normalization, paired initialization and window order. Budgets are 30,000 AdamW updates, effective batch 16, seeds 1000/1001/1002, policy/backbone lr 1e-5, 500 warmup steps followed by cosine, clipping 1 and bfloat16 with strict determinism. Equal updates do not imply equal compute; wall time and peak memory are recorded. The action L1, KL weight 10 and masks match; only Completion adds 0.1×auxiliary SmoothL1. Best checkpoints are selected by prior-zero validation action L1 at shared validation steps, never test success or auxiliary error. Conditions alternate in equal 1000-step blocks; changed source/config/data cannot silently resume.

Pinned official sources contain 250 episodes/83,068 frames. Full synchronized splits are 200 train episodes (Seen_0..7), 25 validation (Seen_8), and 25 offline test (Seen_9). RGB and GT come from the same fresh replay with obs[t]→action[t]→step alignment. All complete expert replays retain their success labels; there is no silent success filtering. The earlier three-episode development inventory cannot qualify as formal training data.

Closed-loop evaluation fixes three held-out garments (Seen_9, Unseen_0/1) and ten new pose seeds (2027..2036). Every training seed evaluates all thirty scenes and four conditions: 360 rollouts. Point seed 2026, cameras, physics, thresholds, 600-step cap and fifty post-success actions match. Pose/scale must match and initial cloth vertices must agree within 1e-5 m. Primary metric is final success; ever success is secondary. Videos, joint actions, checks and checkpoint hashes are retained.

Only a complete paired grid can be ranked. Summaries report garment/seed breakdowns and paired 95% bootstrap intervals, resampling shared training seeds, garments and pose seeds. Three assets and three training seeds constrain inference; no broad garment-population claim is justified. Both Unseen assets appeared in earlier development evaluation, so new pose seeds are not advertised as a pristine new benchmark. Completion includes static and >5 mm moving-point references; short preflight still fails the static gate. If RGB-H itself fails to learn folding, this cannot establish that flow is useless. GT tracks are privileged; practical same-RGB claims require a causal RGB tracker and retraining on its estimates.

Step-3→6 continuation matched uninterrupted training exactly (model maximum difference zero; optimizer/scheduler/RNG/cursor identical); best-checkpoint offline validation reproduced its receipt.
