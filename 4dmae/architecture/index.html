Inside HPT

HPT mixes observation features with learned action and future tokens, producing conditions for the two flow-matching heads.

  • Learned queries: parameters inherited from EgoWAM, distinct from sampled noise and point-query anchors.
  • Generation: HPT runs once per observation; each head uses its fixed context for 50 steps.
  • Two inputs: Tθ encodes noisy tracks; the 16 future-context tokens enter the decoder separately.
Sources and implementation notes

B5 adds point-history input, C/Q completion and shared trajectory-tokenizer weights. The trunk and 64 action / 16 future tokens are inherited from EgoWAM. This run initializes the trunk from scratch and the RGB backbone from ImageNet.

Only action/future output positions are read out. Action inference retains future tokens in the trunk and omits the trajectory decoder. Observed C history stays fixed during trajectory generation.

C denotes points with observed history; Q denotes additional points with current anchors only. PullCube supplies privileged simulator history. The input history includes the current frame; its displacement is zero and is omitted from the 30 output slots.

The action and trajectory heads are flow-matching models. Training losses supervise velocity fields, while sampling integrates them for 50 Euler steps. The trajectory decoder is called separately for C and Q; action inference does not call it. Learned future queries remain in the HPT during action inference.
