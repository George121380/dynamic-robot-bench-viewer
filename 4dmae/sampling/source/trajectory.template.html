What is shown?

Point identities stay fixed throughout a clip. They are sampled from visible surfaces at t₀ and followed backward and forward using saved rigid-body poses. The proposed model input is current RGB, condition history, and query anchors at t₀. Historical RGB is shown here only to make the replay understandable; it is not an additional model input.

Before t₀, blue condition tracks accumulate on the left. At t₀, orange query anchors appear. During the optional future segment, the entire left panel freezes at t₀. Orange query trajectories on the right are reference data, including their withheld past; they are not model predictions. Future reference covers 32 states (1.60 s).

Hollow markers indicate occlusion according to a body-ID and depth check. Off-screen points remain in the trajectory data. Histories use simulator ground truth even through occlusion. Changing the displayed history length is a preview setting; it does not modify a training configuration.

Frame 988 was added because the cube moves visibly in its history window. The earlier three clips are retained. Every source video contains 160 states at 20 Hz. The downloadable example uses frame 988, cube holdout, seed 0, 64 historical states and 32 future states, shown at 0.5× speed.
