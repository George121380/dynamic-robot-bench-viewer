Methods & source
Stratified RandomOne visible point per 4 × 6 image cell; random split into 12 condition and 12 query points.
DINO Diversity6 random points + 18 points selected by feature and spatial diversity. Frozen DINOv2 ViT-S/14 with registers, current RGB only.
Object HoldoutUp to 3 points per visible rigid part. Condition: other entities + 3 static background points. Query: the held-out entity only.
GripperPalm and both fingers form one holdout group. Finger pads are grouped with their corresponding finger.
History16 states at 20 Hz (0.75 s), reconstructed from saved rigid-body poses. Past occlusion does not invalidate oracle tracks.

The held-out object's current appearance and query positions remain visible. Only its motion history is withheld. Colors identify C/Q roles, not physical visibility. The fixed camera exposes very small finger surfaces (10–14 pixels on the left finger); point spacing in 3D does not guarantee useful image separation.

Official demonstration 416 · frames 255, 510, 765 · fixed base camera, rendered at 512 × 512. Sampling uses current-frame RGB, geometry and visibility only; no future state is used to choose points. This is a sampling prototype, not a trained-model result.
