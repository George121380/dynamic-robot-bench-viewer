A pinned deployment of the published simulation pipeline: expert collection, online Gaussian world model, policy training, and closed-loop evaluation. This page separates completed checks from experiments awaiting execution.

Pipeline status

Policy results

Rank checkpoints by success across all 16 tasks. Report the global top-three mean and population standard deviation, plus the final checkpoint. Checkpoint spread does not measure seed variability.

16 evaluation tasks

Eight base tasks at normal and high speed. Each full evaluation contains 40 held-out episodes per task. Failures remain in the denominator; inference exceptions invalidate a run.

World-model diagnostic

Future horizons 1, 5 and 10 correspond to 0.05, 0.25 and 0.50 seconds. Predictions use observations through the current frame; future RGB/depth is loaded afterward for scoring. Equal episode weighting within tasks and equal task weighting globally. No diagnostic metrics have been produced yet.
